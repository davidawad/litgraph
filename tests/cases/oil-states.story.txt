Oil States Energy Services, LLC v. Greene's Energy Group, LLC, 584 U.S. 325 (2018)
Inter partes review is constitutional. Sued for infringement, Greene's petitions for IPR; the Board holds the claims unpatentable; the Federal Circuit affirms without opinion under Rule 36; Oil States wins certiorari on the Article III / Seventh Amendment question. where the pack's shape differs from the record:
  - The PTAB pack forces a patent-owner sur-reply and, after a motion to amend, Board preliminary guidance. Neither existed when this trial ran (2014-15): sur-replies arrived with the August 2018 Trial Practice Guide update and preliminary guidance with the 2019 motion-to-amend pilot.
  - The PTAB pack's own federal-circuit-window deadline (63 days) cannot be computed by the `deadlines` op: the pack's forum 'ptab' maps to no rule set (37 C.F.R. § 1.7 / § 90.3(c) are not implemented). The check above uses the CAFC instance's copy of the same deadline.
  - The CAFC pack tags cert-granted with outcome 'remand' even when the Court goes on to affirm.
  - The CAFC pack requires a bill-of-costs step before the certiorari window.
