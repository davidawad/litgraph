Hughes Aircraft Co. v. United States, 717 F.2d 1351 (Fed. Cir. 1983); 86 F.3d 1566 (Fed. Cir. 1996), vacated, 520 U.S. 1183 (1997); 140 F.3d 1470 (Fed. Cir. 1998)
The 28 U.S.C. § 1498 case that ran a quarter century. Hughes sued the United States for the government's use of its spacecraft-attitude-control patent. The trial court found no infringement; the new Federal Circuit reversed and remanded for compensation; the Court of Federal Claims awarded over $112 million; the Federal Circuit affirmed; the Supreme Court granted, vacated and remanded in light of Warner-Jenkinson; the Federal Circuit affirmed again and certiorari was denied. Exercises both CoFC instances (cafc@cofc for the plaintiff's appeal, cafc@cofc-gov for the government's), the CAFC -> CoFC remand link, and cert-denied resolving through the panel-affirmed flag.

where the pack's shape differs from the record:
  - The first judgment came from the U.S. Court of Claims (its trial division); the Federal Courts Improvement Act of 1982 replaced it with the Claims Court (now the Court of Federal Claims) and the Federal Circuit. The CoFC pack stands in for the predecessor court.
  - The CAFC pack models today's practice (certificates of interest, docketing statements, the mediation program), none of which existed in the same form in 1982-96.
  - Both sides appealed the 1994 judgment. The composed graph only has a government-appeals instance (cafc@cofc-gov) for a plaintiff's win, so Hughes' own appeal rides in as the cross-appeal.
  - The CAFC pack tags cert-granted with outcome 'remand'; here the Court vacated and remanded without argument (a GVR).
