eBay Inc. v. MercExchange, L.L.C., 547 U.S. 388 (2006)
The permanent-injunction case. After a jury finds willful infringement, the E.D. Va. denies MercExchange a permanent injunction; the Federal Circuit reverses that denial as part of a mixed judgment; eBay wins certiorari. Exercises FRCP -> Federal Circuit (cafc@district-court) and the panel-mixed flag through rehearing and certiorari.

where the pack's shape differs from the record:
  - The notice of appeal is one filing but two edges: FRCP's notice-of-appeal-filed, and the CAFC pack's own district-court-file-noa after the link. Both carry the 30-day FRAP 4(a) window.
  - The FRCP pack does not model the permanent-injunction motion (FRCP 65 / 35 U.S.C. § 283) separately from the post-trial motions, so the injunction denial, which was the issue on certiorari, is folded into the post-trial ruling step.
  - The CAFC pack's panel-to-mixed has no remand branch; the real judgment also vacated in part and remanded (the '051 patent).
  - The CAFC pack tags cert-granted with outcome 'remand'; the FRAP pack tags the same event 'pending'.
  - The CAFC pack requires a bill-of-costs step before the certiorari window.
