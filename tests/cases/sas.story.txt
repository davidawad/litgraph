where the pack's shape differs from the record:
  - The PTAB pack's only institution edge is 'Institute trial on all challenged claims', which is the rule SAS itself created. The partial institution that was the whole dispute cannot be expressed, so the walk takes the all-claims edge. Harmless going forward (SAS abolished partial institution) but a pre-2018 history can't be told faithfully.
  - The PTAB pack forces a patent-owner sur-reply and Board preliminary guidance on the motion to amend; neither existed in 2013-14.
  - The CAFC pack's panel-to-mixed has no remand branch; the real judgment vacated and remanded the claim 4 ruling.
  - The CAFC pack tags cert-granted with outcome 'remand'.
