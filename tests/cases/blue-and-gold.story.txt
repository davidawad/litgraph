Blue & Gold Fleet, L.P. v. United States, 492 F.3d 1308 (Fed. Cir. 2007)
The bid-protest waiver rule: a protester who does not object to a patent error in a solicitation before bids close waives the objection. A Court of Federal Claims protest, denied on the administrative record, affirmed by the Federal Circuit, with no certiorari. Exercises the CoFC bid-protest track, the cafc@cofc instance and its 45-day U.S.-party rehearing window, and cert-not-sought resolving through the panel-affirmed flag.
