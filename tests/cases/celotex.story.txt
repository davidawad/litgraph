Celotex Corp. v. Catrett, 477 U.S. 317 (1986)
The summary-judgment burden case. The D.D.C. grants summary judgment to Celotex (and every other defendant) for want of evidence of exposure; the D.C. Circuit reverses; Celotex wins certiorari. Exercises the FRCP summary-judgment -> appeal route added for this case.
packs: frcp-civil-procedure, frap-appellate-procedure (links on)
