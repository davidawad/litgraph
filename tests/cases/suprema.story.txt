Suprema, Inc. v. International Trade Commission, 742 F.3d 1350 (Fed. Cir. 2013), vacated on reh'g en banc, 796 F.3d 1338 (Fed. Cir. 2015)
Section 337 reaches articles whose direct infringement happens only after importation, when the importer induced it. The ITC finds a violation and issues exclusion and cease-and-desist orders; a Federal Circuit panel vacates the inducement ruling; the full court grants rehearing en banc and upholds the Commission, and the panel then affirms. Exercises the ITC pack end to end, the ITC -> CAFC link, and the state flags through a rehearing grant (panel-mixed set, cleared by rehearing, panel-affirmed set) down to cert-not-sought.

    arithmetic: 19 C.F.R. § 210.43(a)(1): 12 days, computed under 19 C.F.R. § 201.14(a) (via § 210.6(a)). The count begins with the first business day after the event: Friday Jun 17 -> Monday Jun 20 (day 1). Because the period is 7 days or more, calendar days are counted: day 12 = Friday Jul 1, not a holiday. Jun 20, 2011 was an ordinary business day; Juneteenth became a federal holiday only in 2021.

where the pack's shape differs from the record:
  - The en banc court decided only the statutory question and returned the appeal to the original panel, which affirmed on 2015-09-14. The CAFC pack has one fresh disposition after a rehearing grant, so the en banc decision and the panel's affirmance are one step.
  - The CAFC pack's panel-to-mixed has no remand branch; the 2013 panel also remanded the exclusion order for revision.
  - The ITC pack's own federal-circuit-review / cafc-remanded nodes duplicate the CAFC pack; with links on, the walk takes the link into cafc@itc.
