where the pack's shape differs from the record:
  - The sources don't say whether the dismissal was with or without prejudice; the pack's only final-dismissal node is dismissed-with-prejudice (granted, no leave to amend), which is what made it appealable.
  - The FRCP 12(h) waiver flag set by filing a pre-answer motion rides along into the FRAP pack. That is correct graph semantics (flags are history), but it means the appellate nodes are the `{waived-12h-defenses}` copies.
  - The FRAP pack routes every panel-rehearing denial through an en banc poll; the docket only records 'rehearing denied', so whether en banc rehearing was sought is unknown.
  - The FRAP pack starts the certiorari window at the mandate; Sup. Ct. R. 13.3 actually runs it from the judgment or the denial of rehearing. The deadline check above uses the rule's trigger.
