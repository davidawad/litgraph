Ashcroft v. Iqbal, 556 U.S. 662 (2009)
Twombly's plausibility standard extended to every civil action. The E.D.N.Y. denies the Attorney General's and FBI Director's motion to dismiss on qualified immunity, and they take an interlocutory collateral-order appeal to the Second Circuit. Exercises FRAP's interlocutory door and the 60-day notice-of-appeal window for federal officers.

where the pack's shape differs from the record:
  - The FRCP pack has one 'motion denied' edge into answer-filed; a denial 'in part' is not distinguished.
  - The Second Circuit's own wording was 'affirm in part, reverse in part'; the Supreme Court's opinion describes it as affirming. The walk follows the circuit's judgment.
  - The FRAP pack routes every panel-rehearing denial through an en banc poll; the docket only records 'rehearing denied'.
