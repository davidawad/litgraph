United States v. Arthrex, Inc., 594 U.S. 1 (2021)
Director review. The Board holds Arthrex's claims unpatentable; the Federal Circuit holds administrative patent judges were unconstitutionally appointed and remands; the Supreme Court instead requires that the Director be able to review Board decisions. On remand the Commissioner, performing the Director's functions, denies review, and the Federal Circuit affirms. Exercises two trips through the PTAB -> CAFC link, a CAFC remand to the PTAB, Director review, and cert-denied resolving through the panel-affirmed flag.
